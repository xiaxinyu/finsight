## 🎨 Logo Concept

### Visual Identity
- **Main Icon**: An eye 👁️ with a **rising line chart** inside the pupil (Symbolizing Insight + Wealth Growth).
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Primary**: Deep Blue (`#2563EB`) — Trust, Finance.
  - **Secondary**: Emerald Green (`#10B981`) — Growth, Insight.
- **Typography**: Modern Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, SF Pro).

### Text Layout
