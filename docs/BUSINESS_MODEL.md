# FinSight Business Model

FinSight’s business model centers on earning trust through an open-source core and building a community and de facto industry standard. Monetization comes from value-added services for users who need advanced capabilities, stronger support, and enterprise-grade use cases.

## Core Positioning

- FinSight is not “a prettier ledger.” It is a local, automated, privacy-preserving financial advisor that understands you.
- Founding thesis: real financial wisdom comes from deep understanding of one’s own behaviors—not from chasing market trends.
- Prerequisite: users must fully trust and control their data. Data sovereignty is non-negotiable and underpins all product decisions.

## Free Edition

- Core classification, analysis, and chart visualization features are free forever.
- Local-first, privacy-preserving architecture with zero cloud storage.

## Paid Capabilities (Professional/Enterprise)

- Advanced Analytics & Forecasting
  - AI-driven financial wellness score
  - Cashflow forecasting and scenario planning
  - Retirement planning simulator
  - Debt optimization and payoff strategies

- Advanced Reporting & Export
  - Tax-ready professional reports
  - Custom dashboards and KPIs
  - One-click generation of executive presentations

- Automation & Integrations
  - Secure, user-authorized “read-only” connectors to banks/brokerages
  - Keys configured by users locally; no server-side data storage
  - Automated statement ingestion to dramatically improve usability

- Priority Support
  - Faster response SLAs and dedicated support channels for Pro/Enterprise

## Pricing Principles

- “Free at the core” remains fundamental—free, open access to essential features drives adoption, trust, and community growth.
- Paid tiers focus on high-impact productivity and insight gains, not on gating basic functionality.
