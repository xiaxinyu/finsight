# The FinSight Manifesto

We believe that **money is not just numbers** — it’s a reflection of our choices, values, and life story.

Yet most financial tools treat us as accounts, not humans.

**FinSight exists to change that.**

We reject:
- Surveillance capitalism disguised as “personalization”
- Cloud-based trackers that sell your habits
- One-size-fits-all budgeting templates

We embrace:
- **Self-knowledge through data**
- **Local-first privacy**
- **Open-source transparency**
- **Financial empathy over judgment**

Your data belongs to you.
Your insights should empower you.
Your future should be clearer.

**Understand your money. Understand yourself.**

— The FinSight Team
