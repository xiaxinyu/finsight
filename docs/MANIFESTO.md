# The FinSight Manifesto
Language: English | [中文](MANIFESTO.zh-CN.md)

> **"Understand your money. Understand yourself."**

## 1. Money is not Math. It is Psychology.
Conventional tools treat personal finance as a spreadsheet problem. They think if you just have enough charts, you will save money.
We believe money is a **behavioral** problem. Every transaction is a decision. Every monthly trend is a habit.
**FinSight is not just a calculator. It is a mirror.** It reflects who you are, so you can become who you want to be.

## 2. You Are Not the Product.
In the age of "free" fintech apps, you pay with your data. Your spending habits are packaged, analyzed, and sold to advertisers who want to make you spend *more*.
**We reject this.**
FinSight is **Local-First**. Your data lives on your machine. It never leaves your control. We optimize for **your** wealth, not an advertiser's ROI.

## 3. Intelligence > Information
A list of transactions is **Data**.
"You spent $500 on food" is **Information**.
"You spend 20% more on food when you are stressed" is **Insight**.
"If you cut dining out by 10%, you can retire 2 years earlier" is **Wisdom**.
**FinSight builds towards Wisdom.** We are building an intelligent advisor that helps you navigate life, not just balance a checkbook.

## 4. Automation with Agency
We believe in automation, but not at the cost of awareness.
*   **Yes** to auto-categorization (save time).
*   **No** to auto-pilot (lose touch).
We automate the drudgery so you can focus on the decisions.

## 5. Built for the Long Haul
Your financial life spans decades. Cloud services come and go. Startups get acquired and shut down.
**FinSight is Open Source and Local.**
You can use this software for the next 50 years. No one can turn it off. No one can revoke your access. Your financial history is yours, forever.
